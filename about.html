Living Culture Philosophy

In an Ultra-Processed World,
We Choose Living Biology.

Industrial beverages sterilize out the magic for cheap warehouse shelf life. At Bramble & Oak, we believe in unpasteurized biodiversity: wild organic acids, living yeasts, and botanical micronutrients working in natural symbiosis with your human microbiome.

Microbiology & Biochemistry

Anatomy of a Living SCOBY Colony

A Symbiotic Culture of Bacteria and Yeast. Our heirloom culture traces back 40 years of continuous fermentation in clean mountain air.

Komagataeibacter xylinus

Beneficial Acetic Acid Bacterium

Synthesizes the cellulose pellicle mat and converts ethanol into restorative acetic and gluconic acids that lower intestinal pH to deter pathogens.

Lactobacillus plantarum

Lactic Acid Probiotic Strain

Clinical probiotic powerhouse renowned for intestinal barrier integrity, bile acid regulation, and resilience through stomach acid pass-through.

In-House PCR & HPLC Testing

Every single batch is gas-chromatography verified to remain strictly below 0.5% ABV.

Zygosaccharomyces

Osmophilic Wild Yeast

Splits sucrose into glucose and fructose, releasing aromatic botanical esters, fine champagne micro-effervescence, and bioavailable B vitamins.

Glucuronic & Malic Acids

Bioactive Organic Acids

Organic acids that bind heavy metals and xenobiotics in the liver for safe elimination, mimicking traditional apothecary herbal tonics.

Our Fermentation Journey

From a 5-Gallon Carboy to Living Taprooms

2018 • The Garage Carboy

First Glass Ferment in Portland

Co-founders Aris and Maya brew their first 5-gallon carboy using organic Sencha green tea and foraged Oregon hops in a cool subterranean basement. The living culture produced an electric carbonation that blew away commercial bottled sodas.

2020 • Farmers Market Lineups

Growlers Sold Out by 10 AM

Pouring fresh iced growler fills at the Beaverton and Portland Saturday farmers markets. Local chefs begin requesting custom botanical infusions for restaurant zero-proof pairings.

2022 • The Taproom Opens

12-Tap Industrial Cellar Bar

We open our brick & cedar flagship taproom in the Central Eastside. Featuring acoustic live music, dog-friendly outdoor patio, and interactive sensory tasting paddles.

2024 • Oak Cask Program

Pinot Noir & American Oak Barrels

Commissioning neutral wine casks from Willamette Valley winemakers to pioneer the Wild Blackberry Bramble and Barrel Reserve #14 series.

Today • 2026 & Beyond

Zero-Waste Bottling & National Cold-Chain

Our solar-assisted canning line packages fresh kombucha in 100% infinitely recyclable aluminum with refrigerated direct-to-door nationwide delivery.
