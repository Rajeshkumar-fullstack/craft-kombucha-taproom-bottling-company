Pasteurized commercial sodas are spiked with isolated laboratory bacterial spores after heat treatment. In raw, unpasteurized kombucha, our heirloom consortia (Acetobacter, Komagataeibacter, and wild Saccharomyces yeasts) produce an extracellular cellulose shield and natural short-chain organic acids. In vitro gastric digestion modeling reveals an 82% higher survival rate through the human stomach into the lower intestinal tract.

Stainless steel is sterile and impermeable, creating sharp, linear acetic vinegar notes. Our neutral French oak casks provide subtle micro-oxygenation over 21 days. This gentle airflow softens harsh volatile acids into vinous, rounded notes while extracting trace wood tannins that stabilize living enzymes without heat pasteurization.

Cane sugar is purely fuel for the SCOBY colony. Over our 21-day primary ferment, the yeast and bacteria metabolize over 85% of initial sugars into glucuronic, acetic, and lactic acids. The finished brew contains only 4 to 6 grams of residual unrefined sugar per 12oz bottle. Natural alcohol stays safely below 0.5% ABV, compliant with non-alcoholic standards.

Our foraging guidelines follow strict 10% harvest ceilings: we never harvest more than one-tenth of newly emerged spruce tips or fir shoots on any single tree, ensuring vegetative growth continues uninterrupted. We harvest on rotating forest permits and leave root systems fully intact.

Because our bottles contain living bacteria and active yeasts rather than sterile dead powders, temperature control is essential. Storing at 36°F to 40°F slows culture metabolism into gentle hibernation. If kept at warm room temperatures, live yeasts resume converting remaining sugars into carbon dioxide, creating over-pressurization and excess carbonation.
