From our cellar oak barrels to your doorstep refrigerator, we guarantee your raw living kombucha arrives chilled at 38°F with active, viable probiotic cultures intact.

1. Advanced Cold Packaging Technology

Every Bramble & Oak parcel is packed in our specialized brewery shipping bay in Portland. We use curbside-recyclable cellulose fiber thermal insulation shields paired with custom phase-change gel refrigerant packs. This creates an insulated microclimate that withstands summer tarmac temperatures and winter freezes alike.

2. Strategic Dispatch Scheduling

We never ship raw kombucha on Thursdays or Fridays. Parcels dispatched later in the week risk sitting in hot, unventilated shipping depots over the weekend. All residential and commercial orders ship Monday through Wednesday morning via priority air or express refrigerated ground routes.

3. The "Arrive Chilled or Free Reship" Promise

We stand unconditionally behind our craft. If your delivery driver leaves your package in the sun, or if carrier delays cause your bottles to arrive warm (above 42°F / 5.5°C), you are 100% covered.

How to File a Cold-Chain Claim in 2 Minutes:

  1. Take a quick photo of the package and bottle state upon arrival.
  2. Submit your order number and brief note via our Contact Dispatch Portal within 48 hours of delivery.
  3. Our cellar support team will immediately review your request and dispatch a fresh replacement cold-pack or issue a full store credit within 24 hours.
