Frequently Asked Questions

Because Bramble & Oak is 100% unpasteurized and packed with living yeasts and bacteria, bottles and growlers must always be kept refrigerated at 34°F–38°F. At refrigerator temperature, our kombucha maintains peak vitality and crisp carbonation for up to 4 months. If left warm, the living culture will wake up and continue fermenting, creating excess tartness and natural pressure.

All Bramble & Oak signature kombuchas are non-alcoholic and strictly contain less than 0.5% ABV by volume, fully compliant with TTB and FDA food safety regulations. We achieve this without heat pasteurization through calibrated sugar feeding schedules and dual-stage gas chromatography laboratory monitoring.
