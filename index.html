The Unhurried Craft

Our 21-Day Living Fermentation Process

Unlike industrial pasteurized kombuchas that add synthetic probiotics after chemical filtering, Bramble & Oak is never heated, never stripped, and never forced.

01

Mountain Water & Whole Leaf Teas

We steep biodynamic whole-leaf green Sencha, white peony, and single-estate Darjeeling teas in mineral-balanced Pacific Northwest mountain spring water.

Rich in native tea catechins
02

Heirloom Mother SCOBY Inoculation

Our multi-generational symbiotic mother culture of beneficial yeasts (Komagataeibacter & Lactobacillus) gently digests unrefined cane sugars into organic acids.

Gluconic, Acetic & Glucuronic acids
03

Oak Barrel Conditioning

Transferred into neutral oak casks for secondary fermentation with whole cold-pressed botanicals, developing micro-effervescence before rapid cold-storage canning.

Maintained at 36°F to preserve life
