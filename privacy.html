Data Ethics & Transparency

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy just as we respect the purity of our fermentation cultures. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Compliant with Oregon & CCPA Privacy Standards

1. Information We Collect

When you place a bottle reservation, reserve a tasting table, or contact our cellar team, we collect only information strictly necessary to fulfill your request:

  • Contact & Delivery Details: Name, physical shipping address for temperature-controlled parcel routing, telephone number for delivery notifications, and email address for digital receipts and dispatch tracking.
  • Taproom Booking Data: Date, party size, seating preference, and dietary or occasion notes for table reservations.
  • Technical Preferences: Theme preference (dark/light mode) and layout direction (LTR/RTL) stored locally in your browser’s localStorage.

2. Payment Processing Security

We do not store credit card numbers, CVV security codes, or raw banking credentials on our servers. All digital payments are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways with end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption.

3. The Fermentation Circle: Zero-Spam Guarantee

When you join our email newsletter, we send only curated cellar dispatches, seasonal barrel release announcements, and exclusive community discounts. Every email contains an instant, 1-click unsubscribe link. We never sell or exchange our subscriber roster with outside marketing syndicates.

4. Your Rights & Data Control

You have the right to request a copy of your stored order records, request deletion of your contact profile, or update your delivery details at any time. Simply send a note to our cellar team via our Contact Dispatch Portal.
